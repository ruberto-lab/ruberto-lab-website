# {% include icon.html icon="fa-regular fa-envelope" %} Contact

The Ruberto Laboratory is located in the [Department of Biomedical Sciences](https://med.fsu.edu/biosci/home) at the [Florida State University College of Medicine](https://med.fsu.edu/) in Tallahassee, Florida.

We welcome inquiries from researchers, students, and others interested in our work, including potential research collaborations and opportunities to join the lab. Prospective students and researchers are encouraged to include a CV and a brief description of their research interests, relevant experience, and the type of opportunity they are seeking.
