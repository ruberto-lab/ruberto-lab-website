We are pleased to welcome Victoria Stevens to the Ruberto Lab as a Directed Individual Study (DIS) undergraduate student!
<!-- excerpt end -->

Victoria is an undergraduate student majoring in Biological Sciences at Florida State University, Class of 2027. As part of her Directed Individual Study, she will gain hands-on research experience working with malaria-causing parasites and contribute to ongoing research investigating parasite biology and antimalarial drug resistance.

We are excited to have Victoria join the team and look forward to supporting her scientific development and research interests. Welcome to the Ruberto Lab, Victoria!
