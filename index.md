The Ruberto Lab brings together expertise in cellular and molecular biology, 'omics, and drug discovery to investigate pathogen biology and treatment response. We study the development, persistence, drug resistance, and therapeutic vulnerabilities of parasites and other human pathogens. Through collaborative and interdisciplinary research, we aim to identify new biological targets and support the development of more effective treatments for infectious diseases.
