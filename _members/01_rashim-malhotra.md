Dr. Rashim Malhotra is a postdoctoral research associate in the Ruberto Lab.

She earned her Ph.D. in Molecular Parasitology from the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research (IISER), where her research focused on the molecular mechanisms underlying gene regulation and stress adaptation in *Plasmodium falciparum*. 

Driven by her interest in parasite biology and translational research, she joined the Ruberto Lab to investigate parasite biology and contribute to the discovery of novel antiparasitic drugs.
